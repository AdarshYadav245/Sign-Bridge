Tips for Maximum Accuracy

  • Hold Steady: Keep your hand still for ~0.5s until the circular progress ring completes.
  • Wave to Say HELLO: Open palm facing camera and wave side-to-side 2–3 times.
  • Nod Fist for YES: Make a fist and bob it up and down vertically like nodding.
  • Pinch Snap for NO: Extend index & middle fingers together, then snap them shut to the thumb.

How SignBridge Achieves High Accuracy

SignBridge uses 3D invariant coordinates relative to the wrist and middle knuckle. This means hand tilt, rotation, and distance do not disrupt geometric recognition.

  • Hold-to-Add: Signs require a brief stable hold (~500ms) to prevent accidental commits during hand movement.
  • Smart Priority: Dynamic gestures like HELLO and YES take precedence over static letters when hand movement is detected.
  • Cooldown Protection: After a word or letter is added, an automatic cooldown prevents duplicate repetitions.
  • Lighting: Make sure your hand is well-lit with clear contrast against your background.
